# Elsewhere cave biomes — first pass

This branch builds on PR #63. It adds seven data-pack cave biomes above the Sundering Horizon, using existing blocks. The biome selection is an initial absolute-height plus vanilla climate/depth approximation, and the terrain cave noise is still the vanilla-derived restoration in #63. The surface, sky and Underhang retain their existing depth channels. Existing ore injection is unchanged. The limestone terrace feature is intentionally a small, coarse prototype; actual basin frequency, water containment and appearance require in-game testing. New biome/feature definitions are hand-authored in main resources, not generated. Run datagen and a clean build to confirm there are no duplicate resources.

## Ant bartering design (not implemented)
Persistent independently sampled personality traits: friendliness, business acumen, generosity, secretiveness, curiosity, loyalty, patience, sentimentality. Separate player-to-ant trust and player-to-colony reputation. Offer budget = sum(quantity × base value × colony demand × personal preference) × clamp((1 + .25 trust + .15 reputation + .2 generosity)/(1 + .25 business acumen), .65, 1.6). Values normalized; demand and preference multipliers bounded. Low business acumen also increases deterministic valuation uncertainty. Secretiveness gates which offers are revealed rather than directly changing prices. Inventory-backed, stable counteroffers; commit atomically, update stock and demand, and persist per-player relationship. No trade reroll by reopening the interface.

## Worker professions (design only)
Professions apply to minor and median workers only; major/supermajor and reproductive castes do not participate in professional bartering. Caste, personality, profession and skill are separate persistent values. Profession selects a stock/demand pool, skill controls quality and trade rarity, personality modifies behavior, and trust + colony reputation gate restricted stock. Minor workers favor foraging, fungus farming, gathering, and everyday supplies; median workers favor excavation, masonry, smithing, apothecary, surveying, recordkeeping, and specialized production. Neither caste is locked to a single profession, and rare specialists can occur in either when appropriate. Example professions: forager (wild produce, rare seeds), fungus gardener (cultures, rare fungi), excavator (stone, minerals), mason (finished blocks, rare architectural pieces), smith (metal stock, equipment), apothecary (resins, utility goods), cartographer/surveyor (cave maps, colony routes), archivist (lore and artifact clues), quartermaster (bulk commodities and colony stock). Rare offers require genuine stock and sufficient trust; avoid trivial progression bypasses. Rare goods are finite, replenished through colony production/imports rather than GUI rerolls; persist offer seeds until stock, input or reputation changes. Profession and rarity affect offer eligibility, not every price directly. No gameplay implementation until ant caste/entity data and trade UI exist.

## Mining compatibility and cave inhabitants (follow-up)
- All 12 existing ore blocks (both hosts) use vanilla mining XP behavior. Cannel/Ferrum/Chalcor 1–2 XP; Electrum 2–3; Corundum 3–7; Indium 3–5. Storage blocks are not XP sources. Keep Silk Touch, Fortune, and correct-tool loot behavior intact; test all variants and storage block round trips.
- Ferrum ingots are members of the conventional c:ingots/iron tag. The 28 vanilla 26.3 recipes with eligible iron ingredients retain their original recipe IDs and outputs, accept either ingot, and permit mixed stacks in separate ingredient slots. Anvils also accept Ferrum storage blocks. Iron compression/decompression, iron nugget conversion, and overlapping vanilla iron armor/tool recipes deliberately retain metal-specific ingredients so Ferrum does not become iron or lose its distinct equipment outputs. Other mods using the common ingot tag inherit compatibility; direct hardcoded iron ingredients in third-party packs are not automatically rewritten. Vanilla recipe data from Misode/mcmeta 26.3-summary; verify recipe conflict priority with other packs and test recipe-book unlocking with Ferrum.
- New pushed cave textures are retained without alteration; registration, models, loot, and placement remain a later cave-content pass. Flowstone/rimstone deposits should produce clustered, irregular terraces; speleothems and curtains need ceiling/floor attachment checks; root strands and fungal mats should be patchy, not uniformly distributed.
- Glowworm concept: small cave-dwelling entities, attached to rock/ceilings, retreat into valid nearby solid-block refuges when players approach, then re-emerge after a quiet delay. Hidden state is persistent and does not permit suffocation, arbitrary block traversal, or duplicate spawning. Glowworm threads are luminous silk/mucus left in the environment, not the actual mob; bounded trail placement and gradual decay, with some natural colonies producing persistent curtains. Distinct rare cave encounters and a glowworm-themed boss are future design, not implemented.
- Flat, empty caves: first diagnose final density and aquifers. Then add biome-specific irregular floors and ceiling relief, pillars, chamber-height variation, narrow shelves, clusters of speleothems and flora, occasional contained pools and connecting passages. Preserve traversal, avoid completely carpeted floors, protect the Sundering Horizon and leave eclogite to its own generation system. No new terrain changes in this compatibility patch.

## Combat equipment differentiation (follow-up)
All four existing broadsword (greatsword) and halberd material variants retain their registry IDs, recipe, texture, durability and repair material. Greatswords use +8 damage above the material bonus, -3.1 attack speed modifier (0.9 attacks/sec), and a 10-tick whack attack animation. Halberds use +1.5 damage above the material bonus, -1.8 attack speed modifier (2.2 attacks/sec), and a 4-tick stab attack animation. Both use normal vanilla attack-charge and critical eligibility; no separate cooldown, reach changes, custom spear charge or additional damage mechanic. Values are starting balance choices; test actual damage, attack indicators, crit timing, sweeping behavior, animation and target invulnerability in-game. Minecraft 26.3 ATTACK_ANIMATION is used rather than removed SWING_ANIMATION.
